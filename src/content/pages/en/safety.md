## Stop exercising and contact a doctor

- pain or pressure in the chest that spreads to the arm or jaw;
- sudden shortness of breath that is out of proportion to the effort;
- dizziness, vision going dark, trouble speaking or seeing;
- sudden sharp back pain when moving, especially after lifting a weight.

## Reduce the load and watch closely

- joint pain above 3 out of 10 during an exercise or the next day (muscle soreness for 1–2 days after strength training is normal, joint pain is not);
- joint swelling;
- blood pressure above 160/100 before a session: do not exercise; at 140–160, only walking and mobility work.

## Always

- breathe out on the effort, never hold your breath;
- get up from the floor or a chair without jerking; if you feel dizzy, sit for a 30-second pause;
- keep a support nearby during all balance exercises;
- do not train with a fever, an acute illness, or in the first 2 days after a vaccination;
- drink water before and after the session.

Your spine during strength work: do not round your lower back under a weight, do not twist with dumbbells in your hands, do not do classic crunches. All exercises in the program already take this into account.
