description: How much protein, calcium, vitamin D and sleep you need to build muscle and bone.

## Protein

1.0–1.2 g per kg of body weight per day. At 65 kg, that is 65–80 g: for example, 2 eggs and cottage cheese in the morning, 120–150 g of fish or poultry at lunch, cottage cheese or beans in the evening. Have a 25–30 g serving of protein within 2 hours after strength training.

## Calcium and vitamin D

Calcium 1,200 mg a day from food: dairy products, sardines, sesame seeds, leafy greens. Vitamin D according to your blood test results, usually 800–2,000 IU.

## Sleep

7–8 hours. Muscles recover during sleep; if you regularly do not get enough sleep, cut back on strength training.

## Creatine

Creatine monohydrate, 3 g a day: in older adults, combined with strength training, it gives extra gains in muscle and strength (Chilibeck meta-analysis, 2017). Discuss it with your doctor if you have kidney disease.
