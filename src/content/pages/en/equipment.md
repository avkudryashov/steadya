---
title: Shopping list
description: Equipment for the home program and what to look for when choosing it.
order: 3
---

| Item                                                                         | What for                                       | What to look for                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Long loop bands (about 2 m), 3 resistance levels                             | Pull-aparts in front of the chest and overhead | Nothing needs to be attached; you hold the band in your hands. Latex or fabric, without handles or clips |
| Mini loop bands (set of 3–5)                                                 | Hip abduction, clamshells, side steps          | Fabric bands do not roll up                                                                              |
| Adjustable dumbbells, 2 of them, up to 10 kg each, or a set of 2, 4, 6, 8 kg | All strength exercises                         | Adjustment steps of 1–2 kg                                                                               |
| Step platform with adjustable height of 10–20 cm                             | Step-ups, calf raises                          | Non-slip surface, at least 60 cm wide                                                                    |
| Massage roller or firm foam roller, 10–15 cm in diameter                     | Upper back                                     | Or a tightly rolled bath towel                                                                           |
| Backpack and books                                                           | Progression of the Sinaki exercise             | Any kind                                                                                                 |
| Shoes with flat non-slip soles                                               | All sessions                                   | Avoid thick, soft soles: they dull your feel for the floor                                               |
| Optional: Nordic walking poles                                               | Posture and arm work while walking             | Height-adjustable                                                                                        |
| Optional: ankle weights, 1–2 kg                                              | Progression of the Otago exercises             | With Velcro straps                                                                                       |

Nothing needs to be attached to a door or furniture: you hold the band in your hands or under your feet.
