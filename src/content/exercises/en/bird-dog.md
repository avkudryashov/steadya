---
slug: bird-dog
title: Bird dog
shortCue: On hands and knees, reach out the opposite arm and leg
image: ../../../assets/exercises/e3-bird-dog.png
imageAlt: A woman on her hands and knees on a mat reaches one arm forward and the opposite leg back
category: core
muscles: [back extensors, core muscles]
equipment: [mat]
steps:
  base:
    description: On hands and knees, reach out only one arm or only one leg
  working:
    description: Reach out the opposite arm and leg at the same time
  advanced:
    description: Same movement with a 2–3-second pause in the extended position
technique:
  - Get on your hands and knees, back straight, eyes on the floor
  - Reach out the opposite arm and leg at the same time
  - Keep the pelvis still, without rocking
  - Return to the starting position slowly and with control
mistakes:
  - The pelvis rocks or rotates to the side
  - The lower back sags as the leg extends
  - The arm or leg rises above the line of the body
stopSigns:
  - Lower back pain as you extend
  - Losing your balance and falling to the side
---

Teaches you to keep your pelvis and lower back still while your arms and legs move, which you also need for everyday walking.
