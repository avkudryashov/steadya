title: Doorway chest stretch
shortCue: Forearms on the door frame, step forward until you feel a stretch
image: ../../../assets/exercises/d5-doorway-chest-stretch.png
imageAlt: A woman stands in a doorway with her forearms on the sides of the door frame and steps forward
category: mobility
muscles: [chest muscles]
equipment: [none]
steps:
  base:
    description: Forearms on the sides of the door frame, a small step forward, hold 15 seconds
  working:
    description: Same position, hold 30 seconds
  advanced:
    description: Forearms placed wider on the frame, hold 30–45 seconds
technique:
  - Rest your forearms on the sides of the door frame
  - Step forward until you feel a clear but painless stretch
  - Open your chest and let your shoulders drop down
  - Breathe freely during the hold
mistakes:
  - The step is too big and the stretch turns into pain
  - The shoulders rise toward the ears instead of relaxing
  - The lower back arches forward with the step
stopSigns:
  - Sharp pain in the shoulder during the stretch
  - Numbness in the arm while holding the position
---

Opens up the chest and offsets the hours you spend with your arms in front of you: driving, at a desk, on the phone.
