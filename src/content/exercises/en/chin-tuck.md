title: Chin tuck
shortCue: Press the back of your head into the floor, draw the chin in toward the neck
image: ../../../assets/exercises/e6-chin-tuck.png
imageAlt: A woman lies on her back on a mat, tucking her chin in and pressing the back of her head into the floor
category: posture
muscles: [deep neck flexors]
equipment: [mat]
steps:
  base:
    description: Lying on your back, press the back of your head into the floor, hold 3 seconds
  working:
    description: Same movement, hold 5 seconds, 10 repetitions
  advanced:
    description: Hold 10 seconds, 10 repetitions, lying without a pillow
technique:
  - Lie on your back with your shoulders relaxed
  - Press the back of your head into the floor without lifting your head
  - At the same time draw your chin in toward the neck, like a small nod inward
  - Hold the tucked position, then relax slowly
mistakes:
  - The chin drops to the chest instead of drawing straight back
  - The whole neck tenses, not just the deep muscles
  - The movement is too fast, with no hold
stopSigns:
  - Pain or a sudden shooting pain in the neck as you tuck
  - Dizziness while holding the position
---

Works against a forward head posture, a common companion of slouching over a computer or phone.
