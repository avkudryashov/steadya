slug: overhead-press
title: Overhead press
shortCue: Ribs down, lower back does not arch
image: ../../../assets/exercises/b3-overhead-press.png
imageAlt: A woman presses dumbbells up overhead, back straight, ribs not flaring
category: strength
muscles: [deltoids, triceps]
equipment: [dumbbells, chair]
steps:
  base:
    description: Sitting on a chair with a straight back
    load: 2–3 kg dumbbells
  working:
    description: Standing, same upward press
    load: 3–5 kg dumbbells
  advanced:
    description: Standing, upward press
    load: 5–8 kg dumbbells
technique:
  - Ribs down, the lower back does not arch forward
  - The dumbbells travel up and slightly back
  - At the top, the dumbbells are above the crown of your head
  - If your shoulder hurts, press at a 45-degree angle forward
  - Breathe out on the effort, do not hold your breath
mistakes:
  - Arching the lower back to push the weight higher
  - The dumbbells drift forward of the body line
  - The shoulders rise toward the ears instead of the arms doing the work
stopSigns:
  - Shoulder pain as you lift the dumbbells
  - Sudden sharp or shooting pain in the lower back
---

You need strong shoulders to take a box down from a high shelf or lift a suitcase into an overhead bin.
