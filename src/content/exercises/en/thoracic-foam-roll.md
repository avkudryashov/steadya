imageAlt: A woman lies back on a roller placed under her shoulder blades, hands behind her head, gently arching back
category: mobility
muscles: [thoracic spine extensors]
equipment: [roller, mat]
steps:
  base:
    description: Small backward arch, without stretching to your limit
  working:
    description: Medium backward arch with a 3-second pause
  advanced:
    description: Fuller backward arch, but pain-free, hands behind the head
technique:
  - Place the roller or a tightly rolled towel under your shoulder blades
  - Hands behind your head support the neck
  - Arch back slowly over the roller
  - Pause at the end of the arch, then return slowly
mistakes:
  - Arching sharply, without control
  - Letting the neck drop back instead of supporting it with your hands
  - The roller slips down to the lower back instead of staying under the upper back
stopSigns:
  - Sharp back pain during the arch
  - Numbness in the hands while holding the position
osteoporosisNote: With osteoporosis, keep the arch smaller and move only until you feel a light stretch, with no pain
---

Restores mobility to the upper back, which for many people gets stuck in a slouched position after years of desk work.
