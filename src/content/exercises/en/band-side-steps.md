title: Hip abduction with a mini band
shortCue: Band above the knees, knees slightly bent, step to the side
image: ../../../assets/exercises/b6-side-leg-band.png
imageAlt: A woman stands with a mini band above her knees and takes a step to the side
category: strength
muscles: [gluteus medius]
equipment: [miniBand, mat]
steps:
  base:
    description: Lying on your side, lift the top leg, no band
  working:
    description: Standing, band above the knees, steps to the side
  advanced:
    description: Clamshells with a heavy band, then steps to the side
technique:
  - Place the band just above the knees
  - Keep the knees slightly bent for the whole set
  - Take small steps to the side, keeping the band taut
  - Keep your upper body upright, without leaning toward the step
mistakes:
  - The upper body leans toward the step to make the movement easier
  - The knees straighten and the band loses tension
  - The step is too wide and the pelvis drops
stopSigns:
  - Hip joint pain as you step
  - An aching pain on the outside of the knee that does not go away after rest
---

Strengthens the muscle that keeps your pelvis level when your weight is on one leg as you walk. When it is weak, the hips sway from side to side with each step.
