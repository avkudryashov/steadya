title: Tandem walk
shortCue: Along a line on the floor, heel to toe
image: ../../../assets/exercises/c5-tandem-walk.png
imageAlt: A woman walks along a straight line on the floor, placing the heel of one foot against the toes of the other
category: balance
muscles: [ankle stabilizers, core muscles]
equipment: [none]
steps:
  base:
    description: Tandem walk along a wall, touching it with your fingers
  working:
    description: Tandem walk along a wall or table, hands free but support within arm's reach
  advanced:
    description: Same while counting backward or naming cities, support still nearby
technique:
  - Walk along a wall or table so there is something to hold on to
  - Follow a line on the floor or an imaginary straight line
  - Place the heel of one foot right against the toes of the other
  - Hold your arms slightly out to the sides for balance
mistakes:
  - Feet placed side by side instead of heel to toe
  - Looking down at your feet instead of ahead
  - Steps too fast, without balance control
stopSigns:
  - Frequently stepping off the line because of losing balance
  - Dizziness while walking
---

Trains a narrow base of support, which you need on an uneven sidewalk or in a crowd where you cannot plant your feet wide.
