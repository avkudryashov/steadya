title: Hip flexor stretch
shortCue: Knee on a pillow, pelvis tucked under, upper body upright
image: ../../../assets/exercises/d4-hip-flexor-stretch.png
imageAlt: A woman kneels on one knee on a pillow with the other foot in front, pelvis tucked under
category: mobility
muscles: [iliopsoas, hip flexors]
equipment: [mat, pillow, chair]
steps:
  base:
    description: Knee on a pillow, tuck the pelvis under, hold 20 seconds
  working:
    description: Same position, hold 30 seconds per side
  advanced:
    description: Same with a slight forward lean of the upper body for a deeper stretch
technique:
  - Rest the back knee on a pillow for cushioning
  - Front foot forward and flat on the floor
  - Tuck your pelvis under without arching the lower back
  - Hold onto a chair if you lose your balance
mistakes:
  - Arching the lower back instead of tucking the pelvis
  - The front knee travels far past the toes
  - Bouncing into the stretch instead of a smooth hold
stopSigns:
  - Pain in the knee resting on the pillow
  - Sudden sharp pain in the groin during the stretch
---

Opens up the hip flexors, which get shortened by long hours of sitting. When they are tight, they pull the pelvis forward and make slouching worse.
