title: Wall calf stretch
shortCue: Heel on the floor, leg straight, a gentle stretch in the calf
image: ../../../assets/exercises/e7-calf-stretch.png
imageAlt: A woman stands at a wall with one leg stepped back, knee straight and heel on the floor
category: mobility
muscles: [gastrocnemius, soleus]
equipment: [wall]
steps:
  base:
    description: Wall stretch with a straight leg, hold 15 seconds
  working:
    description: Same stretch with a straight and then a bent leg, hold 30 seconds each
  advanced:
    description: Same stretch leaning deeper toward the wall, hold 30 seconds each
technique:
  - Press your hands into the wall at shoulder height
  - Keep the back leg straight and the heel firmly on the floor
  - Move your hips forward toward the wall until you feel a clear stretch in the calf
  - Then bend the back knee slightly to stretch deeper
mistakes:
  - The heel lifts off the floor during the stretch
  - The back foot turns outward
  - Leaning into the wall too sharply, so the stretch turns into pain
stopSigns:
  - Sharp pain in the calf or Achilles tendon
  - A calf cramp during the hold
---

Keeps your ankles mobile, which you need for steady walking and climbing stairs.
