title: Band pull-apart at chest level
shortCue: Arms straight, shoulder blades squeeze together, the band is not attached to anything
image: ../../../assets/exercises/a4-band-pull-apart.png
imageAlt: A woman in teal leggings holds a band in front of her chest with straight arms and pulls it apart to the sides
category: posture
muscles: [rear deltoids, rhomboids, trapezius]
equipment: [longBand]
steps:
  base:
    description: Light band, arms straight, pull apart to the sides, squeeze the shoulder blades together
  working:
    description: Medium-resistance band, same movement pattern
  advanced:
    description: Heavy band, 2-second pause with the arms fully apart
technique:
  - Hold the band at chest level
  - Keep your arms straight through the whole movement
  - Pull your arms apart, drawing the shoulder blades in toward the spine
  - Return slowly without letting the band go slack
mistakes:
  - Elbows bend instead of the arms opening straight
  - Shoulder blades do not move, only the arms work
  - The band is attached to a door or furniture instead of held in the hands
stopSigns:
  - Shoulder pain as you pull the band apart
  - A burning feeling between the shoulder blades that does not go away after rest
---

The direct opposite of slouching: it draws the shoulders back and opens the chest.
