---
slug: split-squat
title: Split squat
shortCue: One foot forward, upper body upright, hand on the chair
image: ../../../assets/exercises/b2-split-squat.png
imageAlt: A woman stands in a lunge with one foot forward and one hand resting on the back of a chair
category: strength
muscles: [quadriceps, glutes, hip adductors]
equipment: [chair, dumbbells]
steps:
  base:
    description: Hand on the chair back, short step forward
  working:
    description: Same lunge without hand support
  advanced:
    description: With dumbbells in your hands
    load: 3–6 kg dumbbells
technique:
  - One foot in front, the other behind you on the toes
  - Upper body stays upright, without leaning forward
  - Lower smoothly, front knee in line with the toes
  - Rise by pushing through the front heel
mistakes:
  - The upper body tips forward
  - The front knee travels far past the toes and caves inward
  - The back knee bangs into the floor
stopSigns:
  - Front knee pain as you lower
  - Losing your balance with nothing to hold on to
---

Prepares your legs for uneven support: stepping over a threshold or onto an uneven sidewalk.
