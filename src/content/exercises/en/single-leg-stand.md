title: Single-leg stand
shortCue: Hands on hips, support within arm's reach
image: ../../../assets/exercises/c2-single-leg-stand.png
imageAlt: A woman stands on one leg with her hands on her hips, the other leg raised, a table beside her for support
category: balance
muscles: [ankle and pelvic stabilizers]
equipment: [chair]
steps:
  base:
    description: Next to a support, stand on one leg for 10 seconds
  working:
    description: Without support, stand on one leg for 20 seconds
  advanced:
    description: With eyes closed or standing on a pillow, 30 seconds
technique:
  - Support nearby, within arm's reach
  - Hands on hips, eyes forward
  - Stand on one leg without hopping or bouncing
  - Switch legs after each attempt
mistakes:
  - Looking down at the raised leg
  - The upper body leans far toward the standing leg
  - Grabbing the support in advance, before you actually lose your balance
stopSigns:
  - A sudden loss of balance you cannot recover from
  - Dizziness while standing on one leg
---

A direct test and training of single-leg stability, a key moment in every step you take.
