slug: chair-squat
title: Chair squat
shortCue: Hips back and down, knees in line with the toes, back straight
image: ../../../assets/exercises/a1-chair-squat.png
imageAlt: A woman squats over a wooden chair with her arms stretched forward and her back straight
category: strength
muscles: [glutes, quadriceps, back extensors]
equipment: [chair, dumbbells]
steps:
  base:
    description: Down to the chair until you touch the seat, arms forward
  working:
    description: Holding a dumbbell at the chest (goblet squat) over the chair
    load: 4–6 kg dumbbell
  advanced:
    description: Goblet squat without the chair
    load: 8–12 kg dumbbell
technique:
  - Feet slightly wider than hips, toes turned out a little, weight on the whole foot
  - Move your hips back and down as if sitting down, knees tracking in line with the toes
  - Back straight, eyes forward
  - Stand up by pushing your heels into the floor
  - Breathe out on the effort, do not hold your breath
mistakes:
  - Knees cave inward
  - Heels lift off the floor and the weight shifts to the toes
  - The back rounds when standing up
stopSigns:
  - Knee pain above 3 out of 10 during the movement
  - 'Dizziness when standing up: sit down and wait 30 seconds'
osteoporosisNote: The exercise stays the same; make sure you keep your back straight
---

The main strength exercise of the program. The chair behind you sets a safe depth and
gives you confidence: if your strength runs out, you simply sit down.
