title: 'Static balance: feet together, semi-tandem, tandem'
shortCue: Heel to toe, fingers near the table just in case
image: ../../../assets/exercises/c1-tandem-stance.png
imageAlt: A woman stands with her feet heel to toe, fingers lightly touching a table beside her
category: balance
muscles: [foot and lower leg muscles, pelvic stabilizers]
equipment: [chair]
steps:
  base:
    description: Feet together, arms at your sides, 10–30 seconds
  working:
    description: Semi-tandem, one foot half a step ahead, its heel next to the big toe of the other foot
  advanced:
    description: Tandem, feet in one line, heel to toe
technique:
  - Keep a support nearby that you can touch with your fingers if you lose your balance
  - Place your feet exactly in line, one in front of the other
  - Look forward, not at the floor
  - Hold the position for 10–30 seconds, 2 repetitions
mistakes:
  - Looking down at the floor, which makes balance worse
  - Feet turned out a lot instead of placed in one line
  - Using the support the whole time, not only when you lose your balance
stopSigns:
  - Strong swaying that does not settle within a few seconds
  - Dizziness while holding the position
---

The foundation of the Otago program: the steadier you are in these three positions, the lower your risk of falling on an uneven sidewalk or in the dark.
