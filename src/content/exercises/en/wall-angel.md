title: Wall angel
shortCue: Back of the head, shoulder blades and buttocks on the wall, forearms slide
image: ../../../assets/exercises/d2-wall-angel.png
imageAlt: A woman stands with her back against a wall, arms bent at 90 degrees, forearms sliding up the wall
category: posture
muscles: [rhomboids, rear deltoids]
equipment: [wall]
steps:
  base:
    description: Slide the forearms up and down over a small range
  working:
    description: Full sliding range, forearms stay on the wall
  advanced:
    description: Same range with a 2-second pause at the top
technique:
  - Back of the head, shoulder blades and buttocks pressed against the wall
  - Arms bent at 90 degrees, forearms touching the wall
  - Slide your arms up and down without lifting the forearms off the wall
  - Do not arch the lower back and do not force it flat against the wall
mistakes:
  - The forearms come off the wall while sliding
  - The lower back arches so the arms can go higher
  - Only the hands move, the shoulder blades do not work
stopSigns:
  - Shoulder pain as you slide up
  - Neck pain when pressing the back of the head against the wall
---

Teaches your shoulders and shoulder blades the right position, which you then hold even without the wall.
