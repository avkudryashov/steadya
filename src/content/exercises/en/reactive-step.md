title: Reactive step
shortCue: Sway and catch yourself with a step back
image: ../../../assets/exercises/c6-reactive-step.png
imageAlt: A woman takes a quick step back with her arms slightly raised to keep her balance
category: balance
muscles: [glutes, quadriceps, foot stabilizers]
equipment: [chair]
steps:
  base:
    description: Sway gently backward next to a support and catch yourself with a step, touching the table
  working:
    description: Sway backward without support and catch yourself with a step back
  advanced:
    description: Same forward and to the sides, without support
technique:
  - Keep a support nearby in case you really lose your balance
  - Sway backward and let your body start to fall
  - Catch yourself with a quick step back
  - Repeat the step forward and to each side, 6 times each
mistakes:
  - Stepping too early, before you have actually swayed
  - The step is too small to recover your balance
  - The knees are stiff and do not absorb the impact as the foot lands
stopSigns:
  - A fall where you cannot catch hold of the support
  - Sudden sharp pain in the ankle as the foot lands
---

Practices exactly the movement that saves you from a fall when you trip in real life.
