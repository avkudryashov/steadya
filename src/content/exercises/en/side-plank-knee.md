slug: side-plank-knee
title: Side plank on the knees
shortCue: Forearm on the mat, hips lifted, body in line
image: ../../../assets/exercises/b7-side-plank-knee.png
imageAlt: A woman lies on her side on a mat propped on her forearm, hips lifted, knees bent
category: core
muscles: [obliques, gluteus medius]
equipment: [mat]
steps:
  base:
    description: Forearm on the mat, knees on the floor, hips lifted, hold 15 seconds
  working:
    description: Same position, hold longer
  advanced:
    description: Top arm reaching up, or knees lifted slightly off the floor
technique:
  - Forearm directly under the shoulder
  - Knees, hips and shoulders in one plane
  - Lift your hips into a straight line from knees to head
  - Breathe freely, do not hold your breath
mistakes:
  - The hips sag during the hold
  - The shoulder drifts forward of the forearm
  - Holding your breath on the effort
stopSigns:
  - Pain in the shoulder of the supporting arm
  - Trembling in the trunk that breaks the body line
---

Strengthens the side core muscles. Ordinary exercises rarely load them, but they matter for stability when you walk.
