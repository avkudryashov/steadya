title: Prone Y raise
shortCue: Thumbs up, neck straight, lift 5–10 cm
image: ../../../assets/exercises/b8-prone-y-raise.png
imageAlt: A woman lies face down with her arms spread in a Y shape, thumbs pointing up
category: posture
muscles: [rear deltoids, back extensors]
equipment: [mat]
steps:
  base:
    description: No weight, arms in a Y, thumbs up, lift 5–10 cm
  working:
    description: Same lift, 2-second pause at the top
  advanced:
    description: With a light weight in your hands
    load: 0.5 L bottles or 1 kg dumbbells
technique:
  - Lie face down with your arms spread in a Y shape
  - Thumbs point up
  - Lift your arms 5–10 cm, keep the neck straight and eyes on the floor
  - Lower slowly and smoothly
mistakes:
  - The head lifts with the arms and the neck overextends
  - Lifting too high, so the lower back arches
  - Jerky movement, with no control on the way down
stopSigns:
  - Neck pain as you lift
  - Lower back pain during the hold
---

Targets the muscles that pull your shoulders back and down, countering slouching.
