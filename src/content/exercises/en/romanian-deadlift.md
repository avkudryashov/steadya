title: Romanian deadlift
shortCue: Knees slightly bent, hips back, dumbbells slide down the thighs
image: ../../../assets/exercises/a2-romanian-deadlift.png
imageAlt: A woman with short gray hair in a sage T-shirt leans forward with dumbbells at her thighs, back straight
category: strength
muscles: [hamstrings, glutes, back extensors]
equipment: [dumbbells]
steps:
  base:
    description: No weight, palms slide down the thighs
  working:
    description: With two dumbbells of 3–4 kg each
    load: 3–4 kg dumbbells
  advanced:
    description: With two dumbbells of 6–10 kg each
    load: 6–10 kg dumbbells
technique:
  - Knees slightly bent, keeping the same angle throughout the movement
  - Push your hips back while your back stays straight
  - Slide the dumbbells down the thighs to mid-shin, or only as far as your back stays straight
  - Come up by squeezing your glutes, not by using your lower back
  - Breathe out on the effort, do not hold your breath
mistakes:
  - The back rounds at the bottom
  - Bending the knees more instead of pushing the hips back
  - The dumbbells drift forward, far from the legs
stopSigns:
  - Sudden sharp pain in the lower back
  - Numbness or shooting pain down the leg
osteoporosisNote: With osteoporosis, keep the back strictly straight, use no more than the working-level weight, and go down only to mid-shin
---

Teaches you to keep your back straight when you bend forward, an everyday movement: picking up
a bag from the floor or reaching a low shelf.
