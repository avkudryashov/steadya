slug: farmers-carry
title: Farmer's carry
shortCue: Dumbbells at your sides, shoulders back, steady steps
image: ../../../assets/exercises/a7-farmers-carry.png
imageAlt: A woman walks across a room with dumbbells in her lowered hands, shoulders drawn back
category: strength
muscles: [forearm muscles, trapezius, core muscles]
equipment: [dumbbells]
steps:
  base:
    description: 3–4 kg dumbbells, 30 seconds around the room, shoulders back
    load: 3–4 kg dumbbells
  working:
    description: Same walk, 40 seconds
    load: 5–6 kg dumbbells
  advanced:
    description: Same walk, 45 seconds
    load: 8–10 kg dumbbells
technique:
  - Hold the dumbbells at your sides with a firm grip
  - Shoulders drawn back, chest open
  - Normal stride length, steady pace
  - The upper body does not sway from side to side
mistakes:
  - The shoulders slump forward under the weight
  - Steps become short and uneven
  - The dumbbells swing instead of staying still
stopSigns:
  - Sudden sharp pain in the wrist or forearm
  - The dumbbells start slipping out of your hand
---

Trains grip and posture at the same time. Grip strength is one of the best predictors of overall strength.
