---
slug: clamshell
title: Clamshell with a band
shortCue: Lying on your side, knees bent, lift the top knee against the band
image: ../../../assets/exercises/e4-clamshell.png
imageAlt: A woman lies on her side on a mat, knees bent, a mini band above the knees, the top knee raised
category: mobility
muscles: [gluteus medius]
equipment: [miniBand, mat]
steps:
  base:
    description: Lying on your side, knees bent, lift the top knee without a band
  working:
    description: Same movement with a mini band above the knees
  advanced:
    description: Same movement with a stronger band and a 2-second pause at the top
technique:
  - Lie on your side with knees bent and feet together
  - Lift the top knee without rolling the pelvis backward
  - Keep the hips stacked and the shoulders stacked
  - Lower the knee slowly and with control
mistakes:
  - The pelvis rolls back along with the knee
  - The knee lifts too high and the band loses resistance
  - The movement is jerky instead of smooth
stopSigns:
  - Hip joint pain as you lift
  - An aching pain on the outside of the hip that does not go away after rest
---

Targets the muscle that keeps your pelvis from dropping to one side when you walk and climb stairs.
