title: Glute bridge
shortCue: Trunk and thighs in one line, 2-second pause at the top
image: ../../../assets/exercises/a6-glute-bridge.png
imageAlt: A woman lies on her back on a mat, knees bent, hips raised in line with her trunk
category: strength
muscles: [glutes, hamstrings]
equipment: [mat, dumbbells, miniBand]
steps:
  base:
    description: Lying down, both feet on the floor, 2-second pause at the top
  working:
    description: With a dumbbell on the hips or a mini band above the knees
    load: 3–5 kg dumbbell
  advanced:
    description: On one leg, the other leg straight
technique:
  - Lie on your back, knees bent, feet hip-width apart
  - Lift your hips until they are in line with your trunk and thighs
  - Pause for 2 seconds at the top with your glutes squeezed
  - Lower slowly, without dropping your hips onto the floor
  - Breathe out on the effort, do not hold your breath
mistakes:
  - The lower back arches more than the hips rise
  - Lifting too fast, with no pause at the top
  - On one leg, the knees fall out to the sides
stopSigns:
  - Lower back pain as you lift the hips
  - Sudden sharp pain in the hip joint
---

The glutes hold your pelvis steady when you walk and climb stairs. Weakness here often shows up as lower back pain.
