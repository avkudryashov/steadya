slug: dumbbell-row
title: Bent-over dumbbell row
shortCue: One hand on the chair back, the elbow travels back toward the waist
image: ../../../assets/exercises/a3-dumbbell-row.png
imageAlt: A woman in a sage T-shirt holds the back of a chair with one hand and pulls a dumbbell toward her waist with the other
category: strength
muscles: [latissimus dorsi, rhomboids, biceps]
equipment: [chair, dumbbells]
steps:
  base:
    description: 2–3 kg dumbbell, other hand on the chair back, row toward the waist
    load: 2–3 kg dumbbell
  working:
    description: Row the dumbbell toward the waist with the same support
    load: 4–6 kg dumbbell
  advanced:
    description: Row with a 1-second pause at the waist
    load: 6–10 kg dumbbell
technique:
  - Pull with the elbow going back, not with the hand
  - At the top, draw the shoulder blade toward the spine
  - Do not let the shoulder rise toward the ear
  - Keep the neck in line with the back, eyes on the floor in front of you
mistakes:
  - Pulling with the hand rather than the elbow, the back twists
  - The shoulder creeps toward the ear instead of the shoulder blade doing the work
  - The upper body rocks to help the arm
stopSigns:
  - Shoulder pain as you lift the dumbbell
  - Sudden sharp pain in the lower back as you lean forward
osteoporosisNote: With osteoporosis, always support yourself on the chair, do not lean your upper body past parallel to the floor, and use a moderate weight
---

Strengthens the back muscles that hold your posture and counter slouching.
