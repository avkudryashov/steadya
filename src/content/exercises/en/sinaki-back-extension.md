title: Sinaki back extension
shortCue: Pillow under the belly, eyes on the floor, hold 5 seconds
image: ../../../assets/exercises/d1-sinaki-back-extension.png
imageAlt: A woman lies face down on a mat with a thin pillow under her belly, chest and head slightly raised
category: posture
muscles: [back extensors]
equipment: [mat, pillow, backpack]
steps:
  base:
    description: No weight, arms along the body palms up, lift chest and head 5–10 cm
  working:
    description: With a backpack between the shoulder blades
    load: 1–2 kg backpack
  advanced:
    description: With a backpack between the shoulder blades
    load: 3–5 kg backpack
technique:
  - Thin pillow under the belly, arms along the body palms up
  - Lift your chest and head 5–10 cm
  - Eyes on the floor, neck stays straight, without overextending
  - Hold the position, lower slowly
mistakes:
  - The head lifts higher than the chest and the neck overextends
  - Lifting too high and too fast
  - Adding weight too early, before the second month of training
stopSigns:
  - Sudden sharp pain in the lower back as you lift
  - Numbness or shooting pain down the leg
osteoporosisNote: With osteoporosis, do the exercise without the backpack weight until you have talked to your doctor
---

A back module that lowers the risk of vertebral compression fractures when done regularly.
