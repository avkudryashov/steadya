slug: forearm-plank
title: Forearm plank
shortCue: Body in one line from head to support, belly drawn in
image: ../../../assets/exercises/e1-forearm-plank.png
imageAlt: A woman holds a forearm plank leaning on a table, her body stretched out in one line
category: core
muscles: [rectus abdominis, core muscles]
equipment: [mat]
steps:
  base:
    description: Forearms resting on a sturdy table, body in one line, hold 20 seconds
  working:
    description: Forearms on the floor, knees on the floor, body in line from head to knees, hold 30 seconds
  advanced:
    description: Forearms on the floor, legs straight, body in line from head to heels, hold 40 seconds
technique:
  - Forearms directly under the shoulders
  - Belly drawn in, hips neither sagging nor lifted high
  - Eyes on the floor in front of you
  - Breathe freely, do not hold your breath on the effort
mistakes:
  - The hips sag and overload the lower back
  - The hips rise too high, breaking the straight line
  - Holding your breath for the whole hold
stopSigns:
  - Sudden sharp pain in the lower back during the hold
  - Trembling in the trunk that breaks the body line
---

A basic core exercise that stabilizes your lower back when you bend over and carry heavy things.
