category: posture
muscles: [rear deltoids, rhomboids]
equipment: [longBand]
steps:
  base:
    description: Light band, arms lower out to the sides to shoulder height, shoulder blades down
  working:
    description: Medium band, pause at the bottom of the movement
  advanced:
    description: Heavy band, same movement pattern
technique:
  - Hold the band overhead with straight arms
  - Lower your arms out to the sides to shoulder height
  - Draw the shoulder blades down toward the pelvis, not up toward the ears
  - Return upward slowly, keeping the band taut
mistakes:
  - The shoulders rise toward the ears during the movement
  - The lower back arches to help the arms
  - The band goes slack at the top
stopSigns:
  - Shoulder pain when taking the arms behind the head
  - Dizziness while holding the arms overhead
---

Opens up the chest and trains the muscles that keep your shoulders down and back.
