category: strength
muscles: [chest muscles, triceps, front deltoids]
equipment: [wall]
steps:
  base:
    description: Against a wall, hands at chest height
  working:
    description: Against a table or sturdy cabinet, body leaning further
  advanced:
    description: On the floor, from the knees
technique:
  - Hands on the support at chest height, slightly wider than shoulders
  - Body in one line from head to knees or heels
  - Bend your arms to about 90 degrees
  - Straighten your arms smoothly, without a jerk at the end
mistakes:
  - The hips sag and the body line breaks
  - The head drops forward ahead of the shoulders
  - Hands placed too wide or too narrow
stopSigns:
  - Wrist pain while bearing weight
  - Shoulder pain as you bend the arms
---

A safer version of the push-up: it strengthens your arms and chest without the wrist load of a push-up on the floor.
