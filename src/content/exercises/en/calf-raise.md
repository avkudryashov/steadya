slug: calf-raise
title: Calf raise
shortCue: Fingertips on the wall, heels high, lower slowly
image: ../../../assets/exercises/a5-calf-raise.png
imageAlt: A woman stands on the edge of a step platform and rises onto her toes, fingertips touching the wall for support
category: strength
muscles: [gastrocnemius, soleus]
equipment: [wall, step]
steps:
  base:
    description: On both feet next to a support, no platform
  working:
    description: On both feet on the edge of the platform, heels lowering below the level of the toes
  advanced:
    description: On one foot on the platform
technique:
  - Touch the wall with the fingertips of one hand for balance
  - Rise onto your toes over 2 seconds, lower slowly over 3 seconds
  - On the edge of the platform, let the heels drop just below the level of the toes
mistakes:
  - Rising and lowering at the same fast pace, with no slow phase
  - Bending the knees to help the lift
  - Weight shifting to the outer edge of the foot
stopSigns:
  - Sudden sharp pain in the Achilles tendon
  - A calf cramp that does not ease after stretching
---

Keeps your ankles strong. That gives you support when walking on uneven ground and protects against rolling an ankle.
