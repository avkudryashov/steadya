title: Step-up
shortCue: Step up with the whole foot, step down slowly
image: ../../../assets/exercises/b1-step-up.png
imageAlt: A woman places her foot on a low platform, one hand lightly touching the back of a chair for support
category: strength
muscles: [quadriceps, glutes]
equipment: [step, chair, dumbbells]
steps:
  base:
    description: 10 cm platform, hand on a support
  working:
    description: 15 cm platform, no support, slow lowering
  advanced:
    description: 15–20 cm platform with dumbbells
    load: 3–5 kg dumbbells
technique:
  - Place the whole foot on the platform, not just the toes
  - Lift yourself with the front leg; the back leg helps as little as possible
  - Step down slowly, over 3 seconds
  - The front knee tracks in line with the toes
  - Breathe out on the effort, do not hold your breath
mistakes:
  - Pushing off with the back leg instead of working the front one
  - Fast, uncontrolled lowering
  - The knee caves inward as you step up
stopSigns:
  - Knee pain above 3 out of 10
  - Losing your balance on the way down
---

Mimics climbing stairs, one of the most everyday strength tasks.
