title: Dead bug
shortCue: Lower back pressed to the floor, arms and legs move slowly
image: ../../../assets/exercises/e2-dead-bug.png
imageAlt: A woman lies on her back on a mat with one arm and the opposite leg extended, lower back pressed to the floor
category: core
muscles: [deep abdominal muscles]
equipment: [mat]
steps:
  base:
    description: Slide one leg along the floor and back, hands resting on the thighs
  working:
    description: Extend the opposite arm and leg at the same time, then bring them back
  advanced:
    description: Same movement with a 2-second pause in the extended position
technique:
  - Lie on your back with knees bent at a right angle
  - Keep your lower back pressed to the floor through the whole movement
  - Slowly extend the opposite arm and leg
  - Return to the starting position just as slowly
mistakes:
  - The lower back lifts off the floor and arches
  - The movement is fast and uncontrolled
  - The arm and leg on the same side extend instead of opposite ones
stopSigns:
  - Lower back pain as the leg extends
  - Noticeable strain in the neck instead of the abdomen
osteoporosisNote: Keep the lower back pressed to the floor at all times; do not let it lift or arch
---

Trains the abdomen without bending the spine, a safe replacement for classic crunches.
