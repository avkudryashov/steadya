title: Fast sit-to-stand
shortCue: Arms crossed, stand up fast, sit down over 3 seconds
image: ../../../assets/exercises/c3-fast-sit-to-stand.png
imageAlt: A woman stands up from a chair in a quick movement, arms crossed over her chest
category: power
muscles: [quadriceps, glutes]
equipment: [chair]
steps:
  base:
    description: Stand up fast, you may help a little with your hands, sit down slowly
  working:
    description: Stand up fast without using your hands, arms crossed over the chest, sit down over 3 seconds
  advanced:
    description: Same from a lower seat, sit down over 3 seconds
technique:
  - Arms crossed over the chest, back straight
  - Stand up as fast as you can while keeping good form
  - Sit down slowly, over 3 seconds, without dropping onto the chair
  - Feet hip-width apart, feet under the knees
mistakes:
  - Standing up slowly, which defeats the purpose of a power exercise
  - Dropping onto the chair without control
  - Throwing the upper body forward sharply to make standing easier
stopSigns:
  - Dizziness right after standing up fast
  - Sudden sharp knee pain as you stand up
---

Trains leg power, which declines faster than strength and is a better predictor of fall risk.
