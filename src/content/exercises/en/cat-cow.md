title: Cat-cow
shortCue: On hands and knees, slowly round and then arch the back
image: ../../../assets/exercises/e5-cat-cow.png
imageAlt: A woman on her hands and knees on a mat gently rounds her back upward
category: mobility
muscles: [muscles along the spine]
equipment: [mat]
steps:
  base:
    description: Small range of rounding and arching the back
  working:
    description: Medium range, smooth movement without sudden transitions
  advanced:
    description: Fuller but still smooth range, 10 slow cycles
technique:
  - Get on your hands and knees, hands under the shoulders, knees under the hips
  - Breathing out, slowly round your back upward
  - Breathing in, slowly let your back arch downward
  - Move smoothly along the whole length of the spine, without sharp bending
mistakes:
  - The movement is done sharply and fast
  - The range is too large, causing pain in the lower back
  - The head is thrown back sharply in the arch
stopSigns:
  - Lower back pain when rounding or arching
  - Numbness in the hands while on all fours
osteoporosisNote: With osteoporosis, keep the range of movement small, with no deep bending of the spine
---

Gently loosens up the whole spine before the more active exercises of the posture day.
