title: Diaphragmatic breathing
shortCue: Hand on the belly, breathe in for 4 seconds, out for 6
image: ../../../assets/exercises/d6-diaphragm-breathing.png
imageAlt: A woman lies on her back on a mat with one hand on her belly, eyes closed, breathing calmly
category: breathing
muscles: [diaphragm]
equipment: [mat]
steps:
  base:
    description: Breathe in through the nose for 4 seconds, out through the lips for 6 seconds, 2 minutes
  working:
    description: Same breathing, 5 minutes
  advanced:
    description: Lengthen the out-breath to 8 seconds, 5–7 minutes
technique:
  - Lie on your back with one hand on your belly
  - Breathe in through the nose for 4 seconds; the belly rises under your hand
  - Breathe out through pursed lips for 6 seconds; the belly falls
  - Keep your shoulders and chest relaxed
mistakes:
  - Breathing into the chest while the belly barely moves
  - The out-breath is shorter than the in-breath instead of longer
  - The shoulders rise on the in-breath, creating tension in the neck
stopSigns:
  - Feeling short of breath during the longer out-breath
  - Dizziness during the breathing exercise
---

Lowers blood pressure and teaches you to brace your trunk with your breath before strength exercises.
